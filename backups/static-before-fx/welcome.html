From “I should apply” to “applied” in four steps

Upload your resume once. The copilot does the searching, reading and writing; you make the decisions.

  1. Upload your resume

    It reads your experience, skills and projects, and checks every extracted fact against your file.

  2. Get matched

    Roles come from job boards, company career sites and the job-alert emails already in your inbox, each scored against you.

  3. Review tailored applications

    A resume and cover letter written for each job, with every change highlighted. Edit anything, then approve.

  4. Apply and follow through

    Sends by email or guides you through the company's form, reads recruiter replies, drafts responses and prepares you for interviews.

Questions

Will it apply to jobs without asking me?

No. Every application is prepared and then waits for your approval. There's an optional Autopilot for very strong email matches with a daily cap, and it's off by default.

Can it make things up on my resume?

No. Tailoring can only select and rephrase facts from your own profile. A checker rejects anything it can't trace back to your resume: skills, tools, numbers or years of experience.

Does it work with LinkedIn?

Yes, through LinkedIn job-alert emails in your Gmail. It doesn't log into or scrape LinkedIn, because that gets personal accounts banned.

Where is my data?

On your own computer. Your profile, jobs and emails are stored locally, and the AI that writes your applications can run locally too. If you choose Claude instead, the text needed for each application is sent to Anthropic's API.

What does it cost?

It's free while in early access.
